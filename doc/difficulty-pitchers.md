# Difficulty pitcher characters

The user requested distinct pitchers by difficulty and explicitly approved file changes. Easy keeps the existing white/navy player. Medium is a clean-shaven tan-skinned player in silver grey/crimson; Hard is a bearded deep-brown-skinned player in charcoal/gold. The new characters were created with the built-in image generation tool using text-defined four-pose layouts; they are not recolored copies of the original image.

## Assets and integration

- Easy: `poc/assets/images/pitcher-sprites.png` (existing asset).
- Medium: `poc/assets/images/pitcher-medium.png`; master `source-assets/images/pitcher-medium.png`.
- Hard: `poc/assets/images/pitcher-hard.png`; master `source-assets/images/pitcher-hard.png`.
- Each PNG is a transparent 2:1 sheet with four equal cells: ready, leg lift, release, follow-through.
- The UI sets `data-difficulty` from the engine's selected difficulty. CSS chooses the same sheet for both pitcher and crossfade ghost, so a frame transition cannot flash a different uniform.
- Sheets are preloaded and included in the build manifest. Existing pose timings, animation transforms, day/night grading, aura and game probabilities are unchanged.
- Release anchors are tuned to the extended hand in frame 3: Easy (22.5%, 33.7%), Medium (24%, 35.5%), Hard (20%, 35%). These are local pitcher-element coordinates.
- Difficulty remains locked during a round. Next round retains the chosen pitcher; reload returns to Easy as specified by the difficulty system.

## Medium generation prompt

Generate a photorealistic transparent PNG baseball pitcher animation sprite sheet for a game. Canvas aspect ratio EXACTLY 2:1 landscape. FOUR equal-width vertical cells in ONE horizontal row, no visible dividers. Exactly four full-body renderings of the SAME adult right-handed pitcher, front-facing sports camera, neutral lighting, consistent clothing and identity. Player width constrained to each quarter, transparent padding; no figure crosses its cell boundary. Fixed camera and scale, all planted soles aligned at y=95% of canvas height. Cell 1 (x=0–25%): ready stance upright at center x=12.5%, crown y=2%, feet y=95%, glove held at chest in both hands, feet shoulder width. Cell 2 (25–50%): leg lift pose, body at x=38%, crown y=2%, left knee lifted high toward chest, glove at face, right leg planted y=95%. Cell 3 (50–75%): ball release pose, head centered x=64%, crown y=16%, torso leans forward, throwing right hand extended toward camera and toward left side of this cell at x=56%, y=32%; glove at chest on right side, right toe slightly lifted, front left shoe planted y=95%. Cell 4 (75–100%): follow-through pose, head x=92%, crown y=17%, upper body bent forward, left glove arm folded by chest on far right, right throwing arm drops down in front, back leg lifted and bent toward left side of this cell at x=79%, y=48%, front leg planted at x=91% y=95%. Realistic leather glove and fabric folds, readable at 60–100px player height, full body entirely inside canvas. Actual transparent alpha background, NO floor, scenery, cast shadow outside person, captions, letters, numbers, logos, border or drawn checkerboard. Player identity: tan-skinned adult male athlete with a broad clean-shaven face and short dark hair under a burgundy cap. Uniform: light SILVER GREY jersey and trousers, CRIMSON RED undersleeves and red collar piping, burgundy cap, dark glove, black cleats. Same identity and red-grey uniform in all FOUR poses.
